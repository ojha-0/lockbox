01Chapter One
Identity

The mark is a vault.
The vault is the brand.

Our logo is a wordmark with a keyhole at its center — the letter O drawn as the opening of a safe. It is the single element that must remain untouchable. Everything else — colour, layout, voice — serves it.

1.1 Primary lockup
On Ink 800 · preferred
On Paper · documents
Mono · fax, embossing
1.2 The mark

A keyhole, drawn from the O.

The mark lifts the O out of the wordmark and resolves it as a literal keyhole — circular chamber above, narrow shaft below. It stands alone in every surface too small for the wordmark.

"The mark is what you tap. The wordmark is what you read. They are never shown together at the same size."

Use the mark only at 32 px / 8 mm and above. Below that size, it reads as a dot.

04Chapter Four
Iconography

Outlined, even,
always inherited.

Icons are drawn on a 24×24 grid with a 1.5 px stroke. They are monochrome and take their colour from the text they sit beside. Nothing filled, nothing coloured on its own.

A padlock, on a grid.

Every icon is built on the same 24×24 matrix with 2 px of optical padding, round caps and round joins. Shapes stay open — a lock is a lock, not a filled lozenge.
